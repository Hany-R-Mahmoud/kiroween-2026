# Brand masters

SVG masters of the Trick or Told logo. Copy from these files; do not edit them.

## Files

- `icon-any.svg`: square icon, gold mark on dark, square corners. General-purpose app icon.
- `icon-rounded.svg`: square icon with rounded corners (rx 56). For places that do not apply their own mask.
- `icon-maskable.svg`: mark scaled to 80% inside a full-bleed dark square. PWA maskable icon.
- `icon-small.svg`: heavier mark with rounded corners (rx 48), drawn for favicon and other tiny sizes.
- `icon-black.svg`: mark only, black, transparent background. Single-colour print and stamps.
- `wordmark-gold.svg`: gold wordmark, transparent background. For dark surfaces such as the atlas header.
- `wordmark-gold-on-dark.svg`: gold wordmark on a dark rounded panel. For light or unknown backgrounds and social cards.
- `wordmark-white.svg`: white wordmark, transparent background. For photos and dark one-colour uses.
- `wordmark-black.svg`: black wordmark, transparent background. For light surfaces and single-colour print.

## Colours

- Gold: `#e0a84b`
- Dark: `#0e0b10`

(`icon-black.svg`, `wordmark-black.svg` and `wordmark-white.svg` use plain black and white.)

## Rules

Do not stretch, recolour outside these files, or redraw. Keep the aspect ratio set by each file's `viewBox`.
