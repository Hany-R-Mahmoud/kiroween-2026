Still open: translator credit and public-domain status of the Icelandic Saga Database texts are not confirmed; Forsæludalur and Thorhall-stead chapter wording was read through a summarising fetch, not line by line; Magnússon and Morris Grettir text and the Morris 1892 Eyrbyggja text could not be opened in full; Chadwick's article was not read; chapter numbers follow sagadb and differ in other editions.

Reviewer pass: re-read S1 and S3 on sagadb.org and confirmed Glám the Swede from Sylgsdale with wolf-grey hair, Skapti's advice, Thorgaut, the Fróðá count (thirty servants: eighteen dead, five fled, seven left) and the door-doom brought by Kiartan and Thord Kausi. The S1 and S3 texts match the public-domain Magnússon and Morris translations (1869 and 1892). Removed one design sentence from ch-6.
