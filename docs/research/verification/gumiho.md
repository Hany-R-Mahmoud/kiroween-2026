Open: Guo Pu's peace-omen reading, the Goguryeo fox omen, the set-days rule, the Yuan-era article, the Japanese comparisons and the fox sister outline are unconfirmed beyond reference pages; the National Folk Museum encyclopedias and Sung-Ae Lee (2011) were not readable; the museum's holdings were not checked.

Reviewer pass: re-read S1 (encyclopedia entry E0036434). Confirmed the two types, Cho Hee-woong 1995 with last revision 2023-02-07, and the local attachments (Jo Mok in Andong, Yun Seon-do in Haenam, Yi Sik in Yangpyeong). The entry calls the hundred victims hakdong (school pupils), so "a hundred scholars" was changed to "a hundred students". The entry also attaches the tale to the monk Doseon in Yeongdong; that attachment is left out to keep religious figures out of the legend.
