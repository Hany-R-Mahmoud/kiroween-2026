# Verification: el-coco

Dictionary, chronicle and songbook sources were read as full OCR text from archive.org scans (Covarrubias 1611, Autoridades tomo 2 1729, Barros 1563, Bluteau vol. 2, Rodríguez Marín vol. 1). Sandmann (1955) and Mortoza (2018) were read as PDF text. The Fundación Goya en Aragón page and the NGV essay were read as pages; the 1996 press clipping was read through a PDF text layer. The Prado's own collection pages, the Metropolitan Museum and National Gallery of Art pages, dle.rae.es and the NTLLE historical dictionaries (blocked by an access check) could not be opened and were not bypassed.
