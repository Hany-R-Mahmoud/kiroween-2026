# Banshee fact-check

Gutenberg returned 503 throughout; texts were read on archive.org (Fanshawe, Wilde, Yeats, Cogadh, Gerald, Hull), CELT (Caithréim), Zenodo (Westropp), dúchas.ie and RTÉ/National Geographic pages.

Cromadh page carries no date of its own. |
| 2a. Cú Chulainn washer at the ford | Corrected | Only in the late tale (Hull 1898): "Badb's daughter that ... washes thy gear". Stokes's Book of Leinster text has no washer. | https://archive.org/details/cu31924026824940 | ch-5 retells it from Hull, says the older version lacks it. S17 added. |

Counts: Verified 3, Corrected 9, Unverifiable 3 (15 rows).

Still open: claims resting only on Wikipedia (bean chaointe in Leinster, keening three-part order and two-group structure, bean nighe descriptions, bittern idea, women who died in childbirth); Lysaght's book itself; Caithréim Irish text and Westropp's local Rath Lake legend (read only in Westropp's English); two unread dúchas comb entries.
